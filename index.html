A little practice. A sharper game.

Start with counting, then put basic strategy into practice at the blackjack table.

Keep the count

Start at zero. Add 1 for 2–6, add 0 for 7–9, and subtract 1 for 10, J, Q, K and A. Count every exposed card once.

Convert to a true count

Divide the running count by the unseen decks. This trainer uses exact unseen cards ÷ 52 and truncates the result toward zero. For example, −5 ÷ 2 = −2.5 → −2.

Learn the right play

Blackjack hands use six decks, dealer stands on all 17s, 3:2 blackjack, doubling on any first two cards and after splitting. Up to four hands; split aces get one card each and cannot be resplit. No surrender or insurance. A split 21 pays 1:1.
